  p           observed order. Around 1-2 is a normally converging scheme. Much
              above 2 usually means the three points are not yet in the asymptotic
              range, not that the scheme is unusually good.
  GCI_fine    uncertainty band on the H/15 value. Under ~5 % is a converged metric.
  OSCILLATORY the metric rose then fell (or fell then rose) across the three grids.
              The order cannot be computed, and a small 12->15 change is NOT
              evidence of convergence - the solution is still moving.
  p range     a GCI verdict is only given for 0.5 <= p <= 3.0. A huge p makes
              (r^p - 1) explode and GCI collapse to ~0 whatever the real behaviour.
  B vs B_eq   B is the half-max-lowering span, counted in whole cells. B_eq is eroded
              area / deepest cut, which changes smoothly with cell size. If B oscillates
              and B_eq does not, the B oscillation was measurement, not physics.
