## 6. The next track: a true 1-D (distributed) engine

**Decided by the user:** build a genuinely spatially resolved 1-D model, not a rename. The lumped
engine stays frozen as the **reference baseline**, renamed "lumped 1-D (reference)".

**Proposed design** (plan §2–3):
- **Flow:** the 1-D Saint-Venant equations along the reservoir → pipe → notch → face path.
- **Pipe:** a Preissmann slot for pressurised and mixed flow (alternative: the two-component pressure
  approach).
- **Numerics:** explicit finite volume with HLL/HLLC and hydrostatic reconstruction, and a CFL time
  step.
- **Geometry:** per cell, b, D and z_inv for pipe cells; B, z_b and β for channel cells.
- **Erosion:** the Part 2 laws unchanged, per cell, with Exner sediment transport.
- **Widening:** Part 3, per cell.
- **Roof collapse:** the existing FELA table, per cell, with pipe cells converting to channel cells,
  so there's no hand-over stage.
- **Debris:** carried by sediment transport, replacing the crater box, plugs and H1 rules.
- **Upstream boundary:** the level-pool reservoir.
- **Downstream boundary:** a free outfall, or a tailwater rating.

**Phases and gates:**

| phase | content |
|---|---|
| **A** | Part 6 theory, then sign-off |
| **B** | open-channel solver: lake at rest, Ritter/Stoker dam break, gradually varied flow profiles, hydraulic jump; grid and dt convergence |
| **C** | slot: full-pipe steady flow, surge celerity, filling transition |
| **D** | morphology: exact solids budget; the uniform-flow limit matches the lumped erosion rate |
| **E** | overtopping cases against the lumped reference |
| **F** | piping, P1 against the lumped baseline and the photos, no tuning |
| **G** | back onto the main roadmap (blind set, sensitivity, calibration, validation) |

**Stated in advance:** 1-D will not resolve the 2-D/3-D features (expansion eddies, the arched roof,
debris plugs shaped across the section). **P1 may stay unresolved.**

**Open decisions (plan §6):**
1. The two-track arrangement.
2. Slot or the two-component pressure approach.
3. Explicit finite volume (HLL) or implicit Preissmann.
4. Debris by transport or by rules at first.
5. The package name: `python/onedim/` on branch `onedim`.
6. Start phase A now.

**Lesson carried over:** the 2-D V3.4 engine was stopped on 2026-11-20 after open-ended iteration.
Keep the gates hard, and add no breach physics before the solver benchmarks pass.
